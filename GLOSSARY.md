# MRS Assessors

The public website of MRS Assessors, a legal, tax and administrative consultancy
trading in Lloret de Mar since 2001. The site presents the firm, its practice
areas and its people to prospective and existing clients in Catalan and Spanish.

## Language

### The firm and its work

**Practice Area**:
One of the nine fields of work the firm advises in, such as Laboral or Mercantil.
Each carries a one-line summary, body copy and a list of the work it covers.
_Avoid_: Service, tab, department, area of expertise

**Department**:
An internal grouping an Employee belongs to, such as Departament Fiscal i Comptable.
A Department is not a Practice Area: several Departments may serve one Practice Area.
_Avoid_: Team, division, area

**The Firm**:
MRS Assessors as an institution, including its history and working philosophy.
Rendered in Catalan as "La Firma".
_Avoid_: Company, about, agency, practice

**Office**:
One of the firm's two physical locations, both in Lloret de Mar, each with its own
address and a link out to a map application. The firm's telephone numbers and email
address are not attributed to an Office; they reach the firm.
_Avoid_: Branch, location, headquarters, seat

### People

**Partner**:
A principal of the firm. Three exist: two Founders and one further Partner.
Each has a biography.
_Avoid_: Profile, director, lawyer, boss

**Founder**:
A Partner who established the firm. A narrower kind of Partner, not a separate concept.
_Avoid_: Owner, principal

**Employee**:
A member of staff who is not a Partner. Has a role, a Department and a
work email address, but no biography.
_Avoid_: Worker, staff member, collaborator

**Team**:
The Partners and the Employees taken together.
_Avoid_: Staff, personnel, people

### Content and access

**Article**:
A published piece of legal or tax commentary. Each Article exists independently
per Locale rather than as one piece with translations.
_Avoid_: Blog post, news item, post, entry

**Locale**:
One of the two languages the site is published in: Catalan, the default, and Spanish.
_Avoid_: Language, translation, region

**Portal**:
The external Kabiku application where clients and staff log in. It is reached
from the site but is not part of it.
_Avoid_: Login, dashboard, client area, intranet
