# Open the homepage on a two-tone abstract band

The homepage opened with a typographic hero followed by a full-width treated
photograph of Lloret de Mar at a locked 12:5 ratio. The site's design takes its
cue from co2.is, whose imagery is photography thresholded into two flat colours
so that it reads as texture, not as a picture of a place.

We replaced the photograph with a two-tone abstract of the Cala Bona photograph,
in brand red on canvas, generated by `npm run imagery` into
`assets/imagery/abstract-cove.png`. The source photograph stays untreated. The
statement and the band share one section that fills the first screen below the
header: the band takes whatever height the statement leaves, with a 16rem floor.
As the page scrolls through its first screen, the band zooms from 1 to 1.2 from
its top edge, clipped to the section. On load, the statement rises 4rem into
place and the band settles from 1.1. A reduced-motion preference turns off all
three.

This departs from three rules set when the hero landed (#6): the band no longer
has a locked aspect ratio, it is not under the shared CSS photographic
treatment, and the site now has an entrance motion longer than the 300ms fade.
The rules still hold everywhere else. Text is still never set over the image,
nothing moves without the reader scrolling, and every other design image keeps
its locked ratio and the shared treatment.

## Alternatives

Explored on the `prototype/abstract-hero` branch:

- **The abstract as a full-height backdrop with the statement on a brand block
  over it.** Rejected: it sets text over the image.
- **The statement beside a tall abstract panel.** Rejected in favour of the
  full-width band, which is closer to co2.is.
- **A band one full screen tall.** Rejected as too tall; it took a whole screen
  of scrolling to pass.
- **Thresholding in CSS with an SVG filter**, keeping all treatment in CSS.
  Rejected: filtering a full-width image while it scales on every scroll frame
  is costly, and a generated file is exact and reproducible.

## Consequences

- Changing the abstract's colours or crop means editing
  `scripts/generate-abstract-imagery.mjs` and re-running `npm run imagery`.
- The `first-screen` utility assumes the 5rem header. The interaction
  specification fails if the band stops ending at the bottom of the first
  screen.
