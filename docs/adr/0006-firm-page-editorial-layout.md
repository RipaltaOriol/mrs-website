# Set The Firm as an editorial page over a cloister abstract

The Firm was a single reading column: the long-form title, a treated photograph
of the Girona cathedral cloister at 3:2, then every paragraph in turn. It read
like an old blog post, out of step with the homepage.

We rebuilt it as a sequence of sections. A large serif lede, the founding
sentence, sits under the title. Below it, a two-tone abstract of the cloister,
in brand red on canvas, fills the content width as a band 14rem tall, 20rem from
the `md` breakpoint. Then a large "2001" under the phrase "A su lado desde"
stays beside the prose as it scrolls, and the prose keeps the reading measure.
The two areas of work follow as numbered rows on the mist surface under their
own heading, and the page closes on the last paragraph with a single
centred link to the homepage's contact section.

The abstract is generated by `npm run imagery` alongside the homepage's, from
the same photograph the page used before. Like the homepage band, it has no
locked aspect ratio, is not under the shared CSS treatment, and settles from 1.1
on load. Text is still never set over it, and a reduced-motion preference turns
the motion off. Every other design image keeps its locked ratio and the shared
treatment.

## Alternatives

Explored on the `prototype/firm-page` branch:

- **The treated photograph at full content width under the lede.** Rejected: a
  large photograph that early felt out of place.
- **The cloister abstract as a tall 4:5 panel beside the lede.** Rejected: it
  left a large empty space above the title.
- **The treated photograph, small, under the "2001".** Rejected in favour of
  the abstract, which ties the page to the homepage.
- **An abstract of the Onyar houses as the band.** Rejected: the cloister
  abstract reads more cleanly in two tones.
- **The page opening on the ink surface with the departments listed beside
  the title.** Rejected as an opening; its areas of work and its closing were
  kept.
- **A dossier of key figures and a numbered list of departments.** Rejected.
- **"Client integral" as the label over "2001".** Rejected: the label has to say
  how long the firm has been working.

## Consequences

- The Firm no longer uses `PageTitle` or `TreatedImage`. The cloister photograph
  stays in the imagery catalogue as the abstract's source.
- Changing the abstract's crop or threshold means editing
  `scripts/generate-abstract-imagery.mjs` and re-running `npm run imagery`.
